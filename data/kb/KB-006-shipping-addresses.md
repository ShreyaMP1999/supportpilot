## Adding a shipping address
Go to **Account > Addresses** and click **Add new address**. Enter the recipient name, street address, city, postal code and phone number, then click **Save**. Tick **Set as default** to use this address automatically at checkout. You can save up to 10 addresses.

## Changing the address on a new order
You can pick any saved address, or enter a new one, on the checkout page before placing the order.

## Changing the address on an existing order
The shipping address can be changed while the order status is **Processing**: open the order and click **Edit order > Change address**. Once an order has shipped, we cannot change the address, but you may be able to redirect the parcel using the carrier's tracking page. We cannot ship to PO boxes for Express or Next-day delivery.
