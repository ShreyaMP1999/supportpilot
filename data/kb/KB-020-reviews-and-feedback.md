## Writing a product review
Go to **Account > Orders**, select a delivered item and click **Write a review**. You can rate the product from 1 to 5 stars, add text and upload up to 5 photos. Reviews are published within 48 hours after moderation. Verified purchase reviews earn 50 loyalty points.

## Feedback about our service
To share feedback about delivery, the website or our support team, use the **Help Center > Send feedback** form or answer the short survey sent after each order. We read every piece of feedback.

## Review guidelines
Reviews must be about the product, and must not include personal information, links or offensive language.
