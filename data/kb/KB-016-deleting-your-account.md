## How to delete your account
Go to **Account > Privacy > Delete account**, confirm your password and click **Permanently delete**. We send a confirmation email. Deletion is completed within 30 days, as required by data protection law.

## Before you delete
Deleting your account removes your order history, saved addresses, loyalty points and store credit, and cannot be undone. Open orders and pending returns must be completed first. Invoices are kept for 7 years for tax purposes, as required by law.

## Alternatives
If you only want fewer emails, you can unsubscribe from marketing instead of deleting your account.
