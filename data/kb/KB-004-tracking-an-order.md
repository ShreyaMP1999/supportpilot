## Where to find tracking information
Once your order ships, we email you a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also track any order under **Account > Orders > Track package**. Guest customers can use the **Track order** page with their order number and email address.

## Understanding order statuses
**Processing** means we have received your order. **Packed** means it is ready for pickup by the carrier. **Shipped** means the carrier has the package; tracking may take up to 24 hours to update. **Out for delivery** and **Delivered** are reported by the carrier.

## Tracking hasn't updated
If tracking has not changed for more than 5 business days, or the package shows as delivered but you can't find it, contact support with your order number and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
