## Placing an order online
Browse the catalog, open a product page and select **Add to cart**. When you are ready, open your cart and click **Checkout**. You can check out as a guest or sign in to save your order history. At checkout, confirm your shipping address, choose a delivery option and select a payment method, then click **Place order**.

## Order confirmation
After you place an order you receive a confirmation email within 15 minutes with your order number. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or view the order under **Account > Orders**. An order is only confirmed once payment has been authorized.

## Ordering by phone or in bulk
You can also place an order by phone with our sales team on weekdays, 9am to 6pm. For bulk or business orders of more than 25 units, contact sales@shopsphere.example for a custom quote and volume pricing.
