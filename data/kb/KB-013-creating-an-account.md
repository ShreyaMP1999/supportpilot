## Creating an account
Click **Sign up** at the top of any page. Enter your name, email address and a password of at least 10 characters, or sign up with Google or Apple. We send a verification email; click the link inside within 24 hours to activate your account. Accounts are free and let you track orders, save addresses and earn loyalty points.

## Registration problems
If you see **Email already in use**, you may already have an account — use **Forgot password** to regain access. If the verification email didn't arrive, check spam and click **Resend verification email** on the sign-in page. Make sure your password meets the requirements: at least 10 characters with one number. If sign-up still fails, clear your browser cache or try another browser, and contact support with a screenshot of the error.
