## Editing your account details
Go to **Account > Profile** to change your name, email address, phone number or communication preferences, then click **Save**. Changing your email address requires confirming the change from both the old and the new address.

## Upgrading or switching account type
You can switch a personal account to a **Business** account under **Account > Profile > Account type** to get tax-exempt purchasing and invoice payment terms. You can also upgrade to **ShopSphere Plus** membership for free Express delivery.

## Switching between accounts
To use a different account, click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Sign out**, then sign in with the other account. Order history cannot be merged between accounts.
