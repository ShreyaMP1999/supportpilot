## Viewing your invoices
Every order has an invoice. Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order and click **View invoice** to see the itemized amounts, taxes, shipping fees and payment method used.

## Downloading or requesting an invoice
Click **Download invoice (PDF)** on the order page. Invoices become available once the order has shipped. Guest customers can request an invoice from the **Track order** page. If you need a VAT invoice with a company name and tax ID, add them under **Account > Billing details** before downloading; invoices can be re-issued with company details within 60 days of purchase.

## Invoice errors
If an invoice shows incorrect details, contact support with the order number and the correction required.
