## How to file a complaint
We are sorry if something went wrong. Use the **Help Center > Make a complaint** form, or email complaints@shopsphere.example with your order number and a description of the problem. Every complaint receives a reference number and an acknowledgement within 24 hours.

## What happens next
A dedicated case manager reviews each complaint and responds within 5 business days. Where we are at fault, we offer a remedy such as a replacement, refund, or store credit.

## Escalating a complaint
If you are not satisfied with the outcome, reply to the case email and ask for escalation — your complaint will be reviewed by a customer relations supervisor within 10 business days.
