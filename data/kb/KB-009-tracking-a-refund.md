## Refund timeline
Once your return reaches our warehouse it is inspected within 2 business days. When the refund is approved you receive an email and the status in **Account > Orders** changes to **Refunded**. Your bank or card issuer then takes 5–10 business days to show the money in your account. PayPal refunds usually appear within 3 business days.

## Checking refund status
Open **Account > Orders**, select the order and view **Return status**. The possible statuses are **Return started**, **In transit**, **Received**, **Refund approved** and **Refunded**.

## Refund not received
If the status shows Refunded more than 10 business days ago and you still don't see the money, contact support with the order number so we can provide the refund transaction reference for your bank.
