## Support channels and hours
Live chat is available 24/7 from the **Help** button. Phone support is available on 1-800-555-0199 Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm, and weekends 9am to 5pm. Email support at help@shopsphere.example answers within 24 hours.

## Talking to a human agent
In live chat, type **agent** at any time to be transferred to a human agent. The average wait time is under 3 minutes. On the phone, press 0 to reach an agent. You can also request a callback from the Help Center and an agent will call you within 2 hours during opening times.

## What to have ready
To help us resolve your issue faster, have your order number and account email address ready.
