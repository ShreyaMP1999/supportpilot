## Subscribing
Enter your email address in the newsletter box at the bottom of any page, or tick **Email me offers** under **Account > Communication preferences**. New subscribers receive a 10% welcome discount code.

## Unsubscribing
Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter email, or untick the option under **Account > Communication preferences**. Changes take effect within 48 hours. You will still receive transactional emails such as order confirmations and shipping updates.

## Choosing what you receive
You can choose weekly deals, new arrivals or both, and pick how often we email you.
