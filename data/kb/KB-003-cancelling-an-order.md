## How to cancel an order
Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order and click **Cancel order**. Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is **Processing**. You receive a cancellation confirmation by email and the refund is issued to your original payment method.

## Cancellation fees
There is no cancellation fee for orders cancelled before they are packed. Orders cancelled after they are packed but before they ship incur a restocking fee of 5% of the order value (maximum $15). Custom, engraved or made-to-order items carry a 15% cancellation fee once production has started. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled; please start a return instead.

## Cancelling part of an order
To cancel a single item, open the order and use **Edit order** to remove it. The same fee rules apply to each item.
