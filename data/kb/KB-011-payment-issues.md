## Payment declined
If your payment is declined, check that the card number, expiry date, CVV and billing postcode match your card exactly. Declines are often caused by the card issuer's fraud protection — contact your bank to approve the transaction, then try again. You can also try a different payment method.

## Charged twice or charged for a failed order
When an order fails, your bank may show a temporary authorization hold. Holds are not charges and are released automatically within 3–7 business days. If you see two completed charges for the same order, contact support with the order number and the last 4 digits of the card — never send your full card number.

## Promo code or gift card not working
Check the expiry date and minimum spend of the promo code. Only one promo code can be used per order. Gift card balances can be checked under **Account > Gift cards**.
