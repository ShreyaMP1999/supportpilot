## Resetting a forgotten password
On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password** and enter the email address on your account. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. Click the link and choose a new password of at least 10 characters.

## Reset email not arriving
Check your spam or promotions folder and make sure you entered the email you signed up with. You can request a new link after 2 minutes. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that button to sign in instead — these accounts don't have a separate password.

## Locked account
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes for security. Support staff will never ask for your password.
