## Starting a return for a refund
Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order and click **Return or refund items**. Choose the items and a reason, then select **Refund to original payment method** or **Store credit** (store credit is issued instantly with a 5% bonus). Print the prepaid return label or use the QR code at a drop-off point.

## Refunds without a return
For missing items, parcels lost in transit or orders cancelled before shipping, no return is needed — contact support or cancel the order and the refund is issued automatically.

## Guest orders
If you checked out as a guest, use the **Start a return** page with your order number and email address.
