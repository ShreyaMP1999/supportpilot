## Available delivery options
We offer four delivery options: **Standard** (free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99), **Express** ($12.99), **Next-day** ($19.99, order before 2pm) and **Store pickup** (free, at participating locations). International shipping is available to 40 countries.

## How long delivery takes
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days. Next-day orders placed before 2pm on a business day arrive the following business day. Store pickup orders are usually ready within 24 hours. International delivery takes 7–14 business days, plus any customs processing time.

## Delays
Delivery times start once the order ships, not when it is placed. Public holidays, severe weather and peak seasons can add 1–3 days. Your estimated delivery date is shown at checkout and in your order details.
