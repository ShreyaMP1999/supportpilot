## Our refund policy
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with tags attached. Return shipping is free for customers in the domestic delivery area.

## Items that can't be refunded
Gift cards, digital downloads, personalized or engraved items, and opened personal-care products are not eligible for a refund unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If an item arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, you are eligible for a full refund or free replacement for up to 90 days, including the original shipping cost.
